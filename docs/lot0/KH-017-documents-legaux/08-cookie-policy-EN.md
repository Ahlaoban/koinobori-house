# Cookie Policy

Last updated: [DD/MM/YYYY]

This English version is provided for international visitors. The French version shall prevail in case of discrepancy.

This policy explains how Koinobori House uses cookies and similar trackers on `koinoborihouse.com`.

## 1. What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small file or tracker stored or read on a user’s device when browsing a website. Some cookies are necessary for the website to function; others require the user’s prior consent.

## 2. Strictly Necessary Cookies

The website may use strictly necessary cookies to:

- remember the shopping cart;
- maintain a WooCommerce session;
- secure browsing;
- remember the selected language;
- store consent preferences;
- enable customer account access if this feature is enabled.

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled through the consent panel.

## 3. Audience Measurement

Koinobori House uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics solution: it works without cookies, without individual identifiers and without cross-use with other processing operations. Data is aggregated and hosted in the European Union. In this configuration, audience measurement does not require prior consent.

If the configuration were to change and involve non-exempt cookies or trackers, prior consent would be requested and this policy updated.

## 4. Marketing Cookies

No advertising or retargeting cookie should be enabled in the MVP without an explicit decision and a prior consent mechanism.

If marketing tools, pixels, social network trackers, advertising tracking or retargeting are added later, this policy must be updated and a compliant consent banner must be enabled.

## 5. Payment, Delivery or Security Cookies

Some providers, including Stripe, PayPal or security tools, may use trackers necessary for security, fraud prevention or payment processing. These trackers are managed according to the relevant providers’ rules.

## 6. Managing Preferences

Users can manage their choices through the cookie settings panel (Complianz), available via the "Manage cookies" link in the website footer.

Users may also configure their browser to block or delete certain cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may impair or prevent certain website functions, including the cart or payment process.
