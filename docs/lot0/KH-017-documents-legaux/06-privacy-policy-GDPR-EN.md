## 2. Data Collected

Depending on website use, the following data may be collected:

- identity data: first name, last name;
- contact details: email address, postal address, phone number if provided;
- order data: products ordered, quantity, amount, date, order number;
- delivery data: address, country, carrier, tracking;
- billing data: billing address, information required for invoices;
- payment data: payment status, transaction ID, payment provider used; full card details are processed by Stripe or PayPal and are not stored by Koinobori House;
- customer account data if an account is created;
- messages sent through contact, quote request, B2B/B2G or customer service forms;
- customer review data if the feature is enabled;
- newsletter and consent data;
- technical data: IP address, security logs, browser, language, necessary cookies, language or consent preferences.

## 4. Newsletter and Marketing

Newsletter subscription is based on consent. Customers may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in each email or by contacting Koinobori House.

Koinobori House may send existing customers information or offers about products similar to those already ordered, provided customers have been informed and may object easily and free of charge.

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

## 7. Retention Periods

The following retention periods are proposed for the MVP and must be validated before publication:

| Data | Indicative retention period |
|---|---|
| Orders, invoices, accounting documents | 10 years |
| Customer accounts | Account lifetime, then deletion or anonymisation after inactivity period to define |
| Simple contact requests | 3 years after the last exchange, unless required for legal reasons or disputes |
| B2B/B2G quotes not followed by an order | 3 years after the last contact, unless required for legal reasons or disputes |
| Customer marketing data | Duration of the commercial relationship, then 3 years after last active contact |
| Prospect newsletter data | 3 years after last active contact or until consent is withdrawn |
| Proof of objection to marketing | As long as necessary to manage the objection; internal recommendation: at least 3 years |
| Security logs | Short proportionate period to define; MVP recommendation: 6 to 12 months |
| Exempted audience measurement cookies, if applicable | Recommended maximum lifetime: 13 months; associated data: maximum 25 months |

## 8. Individual Rights

Individuals may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection to processing or data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights: contact@koinoborihouse.com.

Proof of identity may be requested in case of reasonable doubt about the requester’s identity.

Individuals may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

## 9. Security

Koinobori House implements reasonable security measures: secure hosting, restricted access, strong passwords, enhanced authentication where available, WordPress/WooCommerce/plugin updates, backups and limited access to data.

**To be completed in Lot 1:** backup policy, administrator accounts, WordPress 2FA, update frequency, security plugins, incident procedure.

## 10. Minors

The website is not specifically directed at minors. Orders must be placed by a person legally capable of entering into a contract.
