## 2. Data Collected

Depending on website use, the following data may be collected:

- identity data: first name, last name;
- contact details: email address, postal address, phone number if provided;
- order data: products ordered, quantity, amount, date, order number;
- delivery data: address, country, carrier, tracking;
- billing data: billing address, information required for invoices;
- payment data: payment status, transaction ID, payment provider used; full card details are processed by Stripe or PayPal and are not stored by Koinobori House;
- customer account data if an account is created;
- messages sent through contact, quote request, B2B/B2G or customer service forms;
- newsletter and consent data;
- technical data: IP address, security logs, browser, language, necessary cookies, language or consent preferences.

| Purpose | Legal basis |

## 4. Newsletter and Marketing

Newsletter subscription is based on consent. Customers may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in each email or by contacting Koinobori House.

Koinobori House may send existing customers information or offers about products similar to those already ordered, provided customers have been informed and may object easily and free of charge.

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

## 5. Recipients and Processors

Data may be shared only where necessary with:

- hosting provider: o2switch (France);
- software stack: WordPress, WooCommerce, Kadence theme, and the following extensions: Polylang and Polylang for WooCommerce (bilingual website), SEOPress (search engine optimisation), LiteSpeed Cache (performance), Fluent Forms (forms), FluentSMTP (email delivery), Complianz (cookie consent management), Wordfence (security), UpdraftPlus (backups), ShortPixel or Imagify (image optimisation), YITH WooCommerce Wishlist (wishlist);
- payment providers: Stripe (WooCommerce Stripe Gateway extension) and PayPal (WooCommerce PayPal Payments extension);
- email / SMTP / newsletter provider: Brevo;
- carriers: La Poste / Colissimo; Mondial Relay (France and served European countries);
- analytics tool: Plausible Analytics (no cookies, no individual tracking);
- external backup storage: Google Drive (via UpdraftPlus);
- technical providers necessary for maintenance, security or website operation;
- administrative or judicial authorities where required by law.

No customer review tool and no abandoned cart tool are used at launch. This policy will be updated if this changes.

## 6. Transfers Outside the European Union

Some technical or payment providers may involve processing or access from countries outside the European Union (in particular Stripe, PayPal and Google). Where applicable, Koinobori House ensures that appropriate safeguards are provided by the relevant providers, such as standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions or additional measures where required. Brevo (France) and Plausible (European Union) host their processing within the European Union.

## 7. Retention Periods

Data is retained for the following periods, after which it is deleted or anonymised, unless a longer statutory retention obligation applies or a dispute is ongoing:

| Data | Indicative retention period |
|---|---|
| Orders, invoices, accounting documents | 10 years |
| Customer accounts | Account lifetime, then deletion or anonymisation after 3 years of inactivity |
| Simple contact requests | 3 years after the last exchange, unless required for legal reasons or disputes |
| B2B/B2G quotes not followed by an order | 3 years after the last contact, unless required for legal reasons or disputes |
| Customer marketing data | Duration of the commercial relationship, then 3 years after last active contact |
| Prospect newsletter data | 3 years after last active contact or until consent is withdrawn |
| Proof of objection to marketing | As long as necessary to manage the objection; internal recommendation: at least 3 years |
| Security logs | 12 months |
| Exempted audience measurement cookies, if applicable | Recommended maximum lifetime: 13 months; associated data: maximum 25 months |

## 8. Individual Rights

Individuals may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection to processing or data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights: contact@koinoborihouse.com.

Proof of identity may be requested in case of reasonable doubt about the requester’s identity.

Individuals may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

## 9. Security

Koinobori House implements reasonable security measures: secure hosting, restricted access, strong passwords, two-factor authentication on administrator accounts, a dedicated security extension, regular updates of WordPress, WooCommerce and extensions, regular backups (hosting provider and external encrypted backups) and limited access to data.

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, Koinobori House will notify the CNIL within the conditions and deadlines provided by the regulations and will inform the individuals concerned where required.

## 10. Minors

The website is not specifically directed at minors. Orders must be placed by a person legally capable of entering into a contract.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website, providers or legal requirements.
