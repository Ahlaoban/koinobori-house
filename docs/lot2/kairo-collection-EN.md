## KH-KAI-001-075 — La Promesse de la Mer Koinobori - by BCDG

**Short description (EN)**
> A flying carp on a deep night-blue ground, the opening episode of the adventures of Kaïro, an original character signed BCDG. An atmosphere of hope and of setting out to sea. A decorative piece to hang indoors or outdoors.

**Long description (EN)**
> La Promesse de la Mer (The Promise of the Sea) opens the cycle Kaïro des Vents Rouges, Le Navire Sans Nom (Kaïro of the Red Winds, The Nameless Ship), the first adventure of Kaïro, an original character created by BCDG (episode 1/15). On a deep night-blue ground, this koinobori carries the mood of a great departure: the promised sea, the open horizon, the hope of a voyage about to begin. Hung in a garden, on a terrace, a balcony or in a living space, it comes alive with the faintest breeze and lets the story enter everyday life. A traditional symbol of courage and perseverance, the koinobori also celebrates Kodomo no Hi, Children's Day in Japan.
>
> Designed for decorative use both indoors and outdoors, it is made from high-quality nylon, waterproof and UV resistant, made to fly outside season after season. Its rounded mouth, 12 to 15 cm in diameter depending on size, is held open by a reinforced frame. A hanging cord runs from the mouth and ends with a weatherproof stainless steel rotating swivel that lets it turn freely in the wind.
>
> An original BCDG design, signed and released in a small series.
